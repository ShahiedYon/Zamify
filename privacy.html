Zamify Privacy Policy

Effective 31 October 2026. This policy applies to the free, offline Zamify Android study app. Zamify is the app operator. For privacy questions, contact info@zamify.com.

Study data on your device

The app saves your answers, question progress, hints, learning-help usage and study sessions in its local storage on your device. It does not ask for personal details, create an account, collect analytics, show advertising or send this study data to Zamify servers. The app requests no Android permissions and disables Android cloud backup.

Sharing and external sources

Zamify does not share your study data. When you choose to open an AWS source link, it opens in an external browser. Your browser and the destination site may process information under their own privacy policies. Your study answers and progress are not added to these links.

Retention and deletion

Study data remains on your device until you choose Reset local progress in the app, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Reset removes locally saved answers and sessions. Zamify has no server copy of your study data to delete.

Security and future features

The study WebView blocks network requests, does not expose a JavaScript bridge, and restricts external links to AWS documentation. If a later version adds purchases, accounts, analytics or online features, Zamify will update this policy and its Google Play Data safety information before releasing those features.

Contact: info@zamify.com.
