Zamify Privacy Policy

Effective 31 October 2026. This policy applies to the offline Zamify Android study app (v0.9.0). Zamify is the app operator. For privacy questions, contact info@zamify.com.

Study data on your device

The app saves your answers, question progress, delayed-recall review schedule, hints, learning-help usage and study sessions in local storage on your device. It does not ask for personal details, create an account, collect analytics, show advertising or send your study data to Zamify servers. The app requests no internet permission and excludes study data from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

Purchases are not available in this version

This version does not implement Google Play Billing, process payments, verify purchases or restore Google Play purchases. It bundles encrypted candidate expansion content as preparation for a future version. Study progress is stored locally; no payment card details or purchase records are collected by Zamify. Before purchases are enabled, this policy and the Play Console declarations will be updated to describe the actual payment and entitlement data flows.

Sharing and external sources

Zamify does not share your study data. When you choose to open an AWS documentation link, it opens in your external browser. Your browser and the destination site process requests under their own privacy policies. The compatibility screen can also open Google Play to update Android System WebView; Google Play and your browser apply their own privacy policies. Your study answers and progress are never appended to external links.

Retention and deletion

Study data remains on your device until you choose Reset local progress in the app, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Reset removes locally saved answers, delayed-recall queues and sessions.
