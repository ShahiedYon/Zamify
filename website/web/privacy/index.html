Zamify Privacy Policy

Updated 8 November 2026. This policy covers the Zamify website and free web practice, and the offline Zamify Android study app (v0.9.0). Zamify is developed and operated by Shahied Yon, South Africa. For privacy questions, contact info@zamify.net.

Website and free web practice

The website saves your current practice answers, selected choices, progress and use of hints or highlighted clues in local storage in your browser. This lets you resume and review your practice. It does not create an account, sync this progress to the Android app, or send your answers to Zamify servers.

The website does not add advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. Our hosting provider, OpenAI Sites, processes normal web requests and may retain service logs such as IP address, browser information and requested URLs under its own privacy policy. Search terms entered in the glossary are filtered in your browser.

You can delete web practice data using Reset practice on the practice page or by clearing this website’s storage in your browser. Data stays on this browser until cleared, reset or removed by the browser. If browser storage is unavailable, progress lasts only for the open page.

Community voting

If you vote, your browser creates and stores a random vote identifier. We send that identifier and your chosen certification to our voting service, which stores a one-way hash of the identifier, the certification choice and the most recent update time. We use this information to count one current vote per browser and let you change or withdraw it. We do not ask for your name or email to vote.

Aggregated totals are public. Individual vote identifiers are not published. Our hosting and database infrastructure process these requests and may retain service logs. Vote records are kept while this poll is used to plan our study packs. You can remove your server-side vote using Remove my vote in the poll while the identifier remains in this browser. Clearing browser storage alone removes the identifier locally and does not delete the server-side vote.

Contacting us

If you choose to email us, your email provider and ours process the message. We use the contact details and information you send to respond to your request, keep them only as needed to handle that request or meet legal obligations, and accept deletion requests at info@zamify.net.

Android app

Study data on your device

The app saves your answers, question progress, delayed-recall review schedule, hints, learning-help usage and study sessions in local storage on your device. It does not ask for personal details, create an account, collect analytics, show advertising or send your study data to Zamify servers. The study WebView blocks network requests, and study data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

Purchases are not available in this version

The Android app includes Google’s Play Billing library and its required support libraries in preparation for future payments. Purchases remain unavailable: the app does not start a billing connection, process payments, verify purchases or restore Google Play purchases.

The libraries include billing, internet and network-state permissions, plus an app-private signature permission for internal communication. The study WebView still blocks network requests. This version bundles encrypted candidate expansion content; it does not collect payment card details or purchase records. Before purchases are enabled, this policy and the Play Console declarations will be updated to describe the actual payment and entitlement data flows.

Sharing and external sources

Zamify does not share your study data. When you choose to open an AWS documentation link, it opens in your external browser. Your browser and the destination site process requests under their own privacy policies. The compatibility screen can also open Google Play to update Android System WebView; Google Play and your browser apply their own privacy policies. Your study answers and progress are never appended to external links.

Retention and deletion

Study data remains on your device until you choose Reset local progress in the app, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Reset removes locally saved answers, delayed-recall queues and sessions.

Contact: info@zamify.net.
