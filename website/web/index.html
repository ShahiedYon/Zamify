What’s included for free?

All 10 starter practice questions, hints, explanations for every option, a score summary and answer review. Browser progress is saved on this device when local storage is available.

Is this a full certification exam?

This is a 10-question learning set across five topic areas, with mixed practice formats. It is not a full-length mock exam or a prediction of your exam result. Use the official exam guide alongside your studies.

Will Zamify cover more than AWS?

Yes. AWS AI Practitioner is our starting point. More question packs for AI courses and certifications are planned. Titles and release dates will be announced as each pack is ready.

Can I get the Android app?

The Android app is being prepared for Google Play. You can start the free questions here in your browser today. We’ll add the official download link when the app launches.

Where does my progress go?

Your practice answers stay in this browser’s local storage. You can clear them with the reset control on the practice page. Browser progress does not sync to the Android app. See our privacy policy for more details.
