Privacy policy

Who operates this app: Zamify. Privacy contact: info@zamify.net. This policy describes the free, offline Android edition.

What is stored: Answers, question progress, delayed-recall schedules, hints, learning-help usage and study sessions are saved in this app’s local storage on your device. Zamify does not request personal details, create accounts, collect analytics, show ads or transmit this study data to Zamify servers. Study data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

Sharing and external links: Zamify does not share your study data. If you open an AWS or Snowflake source link or the public privacy policy, your device launches an external browser. That site and browser have their own privacy practices; the study data is not attached to the link. On devices with an outdated WebView, the compatibility screen can open Google Play to update it; that store has its own privacy policy.

Retention and deletion: Local progress remains until you reset the relevant exam’s progress, clear the app’s storage or uninstall the app. An exam reset removes that exam’s study records; the original AWS practice has a separate reset. Zamify has no remote copy to delete.

Security and changes: The app blocks network requests in its study WebView. The Android app includes Google Play Billing and its required support libraries in preparation for future payments. They include billing and network permissions, but the app does not start a billing connection and purchases remain unavailable. Before payments are enabled, the policy and Play Data safety declaration will be updated to describe the actual payment and entitlement data flows. Contact info@zamify.net with privacy questions.
